Curtis Tech Solutions — Audio Plugins (VST3)
=============================================

Contents: Strata, VoxNote, Intone, Ducker, Frisson, Verdant (one .vst3 bundle each).

Install by copying the .vst3 folders into your VST3 plugin folder, then
rescan plugins in your DAW.

  Windows : C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  macOS   : ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/       (or /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/)
  Linux   : ~/.vst3/

macOS: if the build was not notarised (see VERSION.txt / the release notes),
clear the download quarantine once after copying:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/*.vst3

VoxNote outputs MIDI from an audio effect: works in Ableton, Reaper, Bitwig
and FL Studio; Logic Pro cannot route MIDI out of audio effects.
