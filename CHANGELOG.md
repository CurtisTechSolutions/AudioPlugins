## 0.1.0 (2026-10-18)

### Features

* **Strata** — polyphonic subtractive synth.
* **VoxNote** — sing or hum into the mic, get MIDI out.
* **Intone** — pitch correction plus vocal chain.
* **Ducker** — sidechain compressor with tempo-synced ghost ducking.
* **Frisson** — nine-knob reward-system sound enhancer.
